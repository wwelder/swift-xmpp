# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, under a CLA that assigns copyright to the project.

That is not boilerplate and it is worth understanding before you send a patch.
This library is AGPL, and an AGPL library cannot ship in an App Store app —
the store's terms and the licence conflict. The projects that do ship one get
there by holding their own copyright and licensing it to themselves a second
time. That option only stays open while the copyright is in one place. A patch
accepted without a CLA closes it permanently, for everyone, including the
people who started the project.

So: the CLA exists to keep relicensing possible, not to take anything from you.
Your contribution stays AGPL for every user, including you.
